DL-TerrainMapper — how to start
===============================

THE QUICKEST WAY — no install, works on a lab machine

    https://dlandscapes.github.io/dl-terrain-mapper/

  Open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. That is the whole setup.


FROM THIS FOLDER, ON YOUR OWN MACHINE

  Double-click        start.bat

  Your browser opens with the tool in it. Leave the small black console
  window alone while you work — closing it stops the tool.

  You need Python 3 installed. There is nothing to install beyond that:
  no pip, no npm, no build step.


*** DO NOT double-click project\index.html ***

  It will open, and it will be BLANK. That is not a broken download.
  Browsers refuse to load this kind of page straight off the disk, for a
  security reason that has nothing to do with this tool. Use start.bat,
  or the web link above.


FIRST FIVE MINUTES

  1. Press "or build a demonstration site from nothing".
     A landform appears with contours on it. You need no data for this.

  2. Open the "Compose" section on the left. Set your bed size, then press
     "Pick the largest scale that fits".

  3. Open the properties window on the right. Everything there applies to
     the raster selected in the layer list. Try:
        Hachures                 — strokes down the slope
        Sections                 — three cuts through the plate
        Change as circles        — a grading plan's cut and fill
        Change as hatching       — tone, as marks

  4. Read the panel under "Readout". It tells you how many separate marks
     the drawing became. That number is your job time.

  5. "Export" writes the DXF and SVG, plus a plain-text cutting report.


YOUR FILES STAY ON YOUR COMPUTER

  Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload to. Photographs
  in particular are read inside your own browser tab and never sent.


AT THE MACHINE

  - DLF-99_sheet is a boundary. Assign it to NO pass.
  - Cut passes run LAST.
  - No power or speed values ship with this tool — they belong to your
    machine and your material. Cut the material test sheet first.
